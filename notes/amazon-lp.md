## Notes on framing for a research profile

- **Customer Obsession** — who is the "customer" of your research? Clinicians, patients, the
  downstream team using your model. Frame impact in their terms, not the paper's.
- **Dive Deep** — your natural strength; a debugging-a-model or data-quality story lands well.
- **Bias for Action** — counter the "PhDs over-analyze" prior: a time you shipped a good-enough
  result under a deadline instead of chasing the perfect one.
- **Invent and Simplify** — a method you simplified, or a complex pipeline you cut down.
- **Earn Trust** — a reviewer/collaborator disagreement you resolved without ego.
- **Are Right, A Lot** — a judgment call that looked wrong at the time and paid off (or that you
  corrected quickly when the data said otherwise — that also demonstrates the LP).
