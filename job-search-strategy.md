# Job Search Strategy

_Last updated: July 2026_

## Locked decision: graduate May 2027

Single graduation date used on **every** application: **May 2027**. No contingent dates, no per-application variation, no mid-cycle flips.

Reason for the choice: being done sooner is the priority. This is a valid driver on its own. It does not improve job odds, and it is worth being clear-eyed that it trades away the strongest big-tech entry (the internship to return-offer path) in exchange for a simpler, fully honest timeline. That trade was made deliberately.

What May 2027 buys:
- One honest date on everything. No advisor-dependent pushing, no contingency to manage, no inconsistency risk across a candidate profile.
- A clean "available summer 2027, no strings" story for strong-fit employers.
- On-time access to the fall-2026 big-tech new-grad full-time pipeline (the second-best big-tech door, but a real structured one).

What it costs:
- The big-tech **internship** door closes. Graduating before a Summer 2027 internship starts means no returning-student status, which is what most big-tech intern reqs screen on.

## The two tracks

### Track A (core, highest hit rate): strong-fit full-time
Health-tech, clinical NLP, and mid-size applied-ML companies where the production pipeline (HALO/BIDMC) and VAERS work map directly onto the job description. Cold full-time applications actually convert here: the pool is thinner and domain-filtered, and many of these firms don't run big internship programs, so full-time is their primary hiring path.

- **Timing:** spring 2027, a few months ahead of a summer-2027 start. This is their real hiring window. Fall is too early for them regardless of graduation date, because they hire to fill seats within a few months.
- **Effort:** this is where the careful tailoring goes. Flowing "why this company" essays and cover letters, domain-specific framing, research deep-dives.
- **Fall 2026 use of this track:** build the target list, identify which firms are worth applying to, and warm up referrals so spring applications land on a desk that already knows the name. No premature applications.

### Track B (reach): big-tech new-grad full-time
Applied scientist and applied-ML new-grad roles at large firms. Higher bar, narrower funnel, and a chunk of headcount already claimed by converting interns. Real but low-odds.

- **Timing:** fall 2026, on time with the structured new-grad pipeline. This is the whole point of committing to May early: it lets this pipeline be worked properly rather than arriving late in spring.
- **Effort:** strong-but-efficient applications across the labs. The bottleneck is being early and being in the pile. Referrals matter more here than almost any other lever.

## Ranking of big-tech doors (best to worst)
1. Intern to return-offer — **closed** by the May 2027 date.
2. Fall new-grad full-time pipeline — **open**, worked in fall 2026. This is now the primary big-tech shot.
3. Spring cold full-time — worst door, late into a filled cycle. Not part of the plan.

## Core principles (carry across every application)
- **One date everywhere:** May 2027, no exceptions.
- **No overclaiming domain adjacency:** position honestly around LLM, foundation-model, and production-pipeline strengths. Do not claim connection to domains where the background doesn't genuinely apply (CV/perception, autonomous driving, oncology, etc.).
- **Method-first reframing:** describe the portfolio by its methods, not its domains, to read as a reasoning-systems person (see target-roles.md). MERMAID, the generator-verifier temporal work, and FAIR-Q are multi-agent, verifier-guided, iterative-refinement systems. Clinical and veracity are application domains, not the headline.
- **Get preprints up:** the moment papers 3 and 4 are submittable, put them on arXiv so "under review at [venue]" points to something a recruiter can open. Neither will have an acceptance decision back during fall screening, so the preprint is what carries the signal.
- **RL is aspirational, not this cycle:** no RL track record yet. "LLM reasoning and multi-agent systems" is the honest banner. Treat RL as a longer play (a real project or a reasoning paper that touches RL-style training), not a Summer 2027 application story.
- **Referrals beat cold applications**, especially for research and big-tech roles.
