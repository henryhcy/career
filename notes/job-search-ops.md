# Job search operations — sourcing & applying

The board (`applications.json`) tracks roles once you have them. This is how you **find** them
and keep the pipeline fed. Rule: log every role on the board the **same day** you find it
(status `lead`), move it to `applied` the day you apply.

## Where to look (check weekly; set alerts)

- **Company career pages — apply on the source, not a mirror:** Amazon Jobs (+ **Amazon Science**
  for Applied Scientist reqs), Google Careers, Meta Careers, Bloomberg Careers, TikTok / ByteDance
  Careers.
- **LinkedIn Jobs** — saved searches + alerts for "Applied Scientist" and "Machine Learning
  Engineer", filtered to NYC.
- **New-grad / internship aggregators** (GitHub new-grad lists, Simplify, etc.) — best for
  catching reqs the week they open.
- **University career center**, PhD-industry pipelines, department + advisor network.
- **Levels.fyi** for comp benchmarks — feeds the `companies/*.md` comp tables.

## Timing (verify each — postings move year to year)

- **Fall 2026 internships:** many are rolling — apply ASAP. The Jul 26 milestone is 10–12 out.
- **New-grad / FT 2027 reqs:** big-tech typically opens late summer → fall. Watch for them; the
  goal is 15+ FT apps by Aug 31, **front-loaded** so callbacks land while you defend.
- Run a **weekly sourcing sweep** (part of the Friday routine): scan alerts, log new leads.

## Filters to apply every time

- **Location:** NYC-first (the roadmap targets), plus any remote/relocation you'd genuinely take.
- **Sponsorship:** F-1 requiring H-1B — confirm the company/posting sponsors *before* investing
  time. Board notes carry a sponsorship marker for exactly this reason; four Track A targets are
  still unverified (Abridge, Komodo Health, Insitro, Scale AI).
- **Level:** new-grad full-time, matched to a **May 2027** graduation. **Not intern** — that door
  is closed by the May date (see job-search-strategy.md).

## The referral sweep (highest-leverage — done by Oct 1)

1. For each target company, list 1st/2nd-degree connections: LinkedIn, alumni, co-authors, labmates.
2. Reach out with the referral one-pager (see [resume-prep.md](resume-prep.md)) — one clear ask each.
3. A referral beats a cold apply almost everywhere — prioritize companies where you have a path in.
4. Record who referred you in the card's `source` field so you know who to thank.

This is what the daily **outreach** habit feeds; the Oct 1 milestone marks it "done".

## Application hygiene

- Log the role the day you find it (`lead`); move to `applied` the day you apply.
- Tailor resume keywords + write the cover letter per role (cover letters live in `career-docs`,
  one per company — see the workflow in the repo README/CLAUDE.md).
- Put real deadlines in the card's `nextStepDate`; the board turns a card red after 10 quiet days.
- After any reply, the card's "last touch" updates when you drag it — keep staleness honest.
